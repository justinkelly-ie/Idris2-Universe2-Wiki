# 🌀 Discrete Noether's Theorem & Symplectic Invariants

In continuous mechanics, Noether's theorem connects continuous symmetries of the Lagrangian with conserved physical currents:
$$\delta L = 0 \implies \frac{d}{dt} \left( \frac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{q}} \delta q \right) = 0$$

In **Idris2-Universe2**, exact conservation laws hold on discrete lattices without time-step limits through **Discrete Symplectic Mechanics** and **Multiset Phase Spaces**.

## 🏛️ 1. Discrete Symplectic Formulation

1. **Symplectic Form Conservation**:
   A discrete time-step map $\Phi : (q_n, p_n) \mapsto (q_{n+1}, p_{n+1})$ is symplectic if and only if it preserves the 2-form area multiset:
   $$dq_{n+1} \wedge dp_{n+1} \equiv dq_n \wedge dp_n$$
   In our multiset wedge algebra, this is guaranteed by the canonical symplectic matrix Maxel $J = \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$.

2. **Discrete Noether Charge**:
   Under a discrete spatial symmetry generated by a permutation or shift matrix $P_\sigma \in \text{Maxel}$, the discrete momentum contraction:
   $$Q = p^T \cdot (\delta q) \in \text{BoxInt}$$
   is an exact invariant across all simulation epochs.
